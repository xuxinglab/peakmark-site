Support

Last updated: October 29, 2026

For help, email xuxing.lab@gmail.com.

What we can help with

  • Purchases and Restore Purchase: Issues with the Lifetime in-app purchase or restoring it with the same Apple Account.
  • iCloud sync: Sync settings, account status, sync failures, and questions about data in your private iCloud database. Sync only works between devices signed in to the same Apple Account; when you switch to a new iPhone, turn on sync in the app's settings on the new phone. If a device signs in to a different Apple Account, the app stops syncing to protect the original account's records. If you need to change accounts, please email us first and do not delete the app.
  • Records and photos: Adding or editing records, choosing photos, and processing stickers.
  • General feedback: Bug reports and suggestions.

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send hiking photos, notes, precise coordinates, Apple Account passwords, or payment information unless it is necessary and you choose to share them.
